I added `gigasecond::advance`, which takes a `boost::posix_time::ptime` and returns it plus 1,000,000,000 seconds. A small local check gave the expected results:
- 2015-01-24 22:00:00 → 2046-Oct-02 23:46:40 (the example from the instructions)
- 2011-04-25 00:00:00 → 2043-Jan-01 01:46:40
- 1959-07-19 23:59:59 → 1991-Mar-28 01:46:39
