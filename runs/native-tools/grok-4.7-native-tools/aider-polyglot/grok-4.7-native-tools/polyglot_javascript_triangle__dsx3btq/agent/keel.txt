The `Triangle` class now classifies a shape from its three side lengths.

- It is a valid triangle only when every side is greater than 0 and the triangle inequality holds (`a + b ≥ c` and the two rotations).
- Equilateral means all three sides are equal.
- Isosceles means at least two sides are equal, so equilateral triangles count.
- Scalene means all three sides differ.
- Invalid triangles match none of those.
